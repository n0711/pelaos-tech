# PELAOS Tech - website pilot customer validation
17 November 2026 | Internal planning, not a sales or delivery claim

## Goal and evidence boundary
Test whether a small business will request and accept a **written, fixed-scope landing-page quote** before committing to subscriptions, checkout software or new product development. The public site is an informational portfolio, not a sales/payment platform. No customer enquiries, revenue or bookings are asserted by this plan.

## One initial offer
Offer a responsive landing page with business-approved text and images, contact links, basic on-page search/share metadata, one consolidated revision and source handover. Price, tax, hosting, launch date and conditions must be confirmed in a written quote. The €250 example in [SERVICE_OFFER.md](SERVICE_OFFER.md) is an experimental estimate, **not** a currently advertised price or commitment.

## Discovery segments (worldwide)
Start with **one** segment for the first batch, then compare results before expanding:
- Independent skate, surf and sports shops with an outdated or hard-to-use public information site
- Small specialist repair or equipment services that need a clear services/contact page
- Research and engineering teams that need a simple public project or lab information page

Choose businesses based on an observed public site problem, not location alone. Avoid claiming any named business needs a redesign without checking.

## Qualification workflow
1. **Observe** a specific, public website problem. Record only the business name and public website in a private worksheet; do not publish personal contact data in Git.
2. **Ask permission** for a short conversation about an informational website. Do not bulk-send unsolicited email or post automated messages.
3. **Discover** the visitor goal, desired pages, deadline, required language, existing content, hosting preference and decision-maker.
4. **Quote** only after clarifying what is in/out of scope, costs, revisions, accessibility requirements, handover and cancellation terms.
5. **Record** whether the quote was accepted, declined or deferred, the reason, and the actual time spent (including sales time).

## Validation targets (hypotheses, not results)
- Research **10** relevant businesses in one segment from their public sites.
- Hold up to **5** voluntary discovery conversations.
- Aim for **2** adequately specified briefs and **1** written quote request.
- Record actual numbers rather than backfilling goals as achievements.

Track: date, source, segment, observed problem, stage, reasons for declining, quote size, *total* time, site delivery effort and any real payment. Keep lead identities, contact details and agreements out of the public repository.

## Decision at the end of the batch
- **Continue:** a qualified prospect requests a written quote at a scope and price that appear feasible.
- **Revise:** prospects want other features, a different business segment or more evidence before a quote.
- **Pause:** no meaningful interest or the delivery effort would exceed the proposed price.

Do not claim that a page view is a lead or that an enquiry is a sale. Do not purchase domains, subscriptions or advertising for this validation without a concrete business reason and approval.

## Delivery gate
Before taking payment: agree a written scope, identify permitted assets, confirm applicable business/tax responsibilities, verify hosting permits commercial activity, and define how work is approved and handed over. The GitHub Pages information site should not become an e-commerce storefront under this pilot.
