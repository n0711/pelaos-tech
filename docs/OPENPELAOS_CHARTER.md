# OpenPELAOS charter
26 October 2026 | Draft v0.1 | For review; not adopted

OpenPELAOS is proposed as the civilian research and shared-software strand of PELAOS. Its purpose is to make useful engineering work inspectable and reproducible, beginning with marine and field robotics, telemetry observation and the tools needed to examine evidence.

ROS3-Limassol is the first research project covered by this charter draft. It explores receiver-arrival liveness under intermittent connectivity. Results must identify the code revision, environment, input data and method used. Synthetic experiments, bench tests and field observations must be labelled separately. Recent accepted arrivals do not by themselves establish sensor freshness, communication delay, vehicle health or safety.

Contributions should include a small reproducible example, stated assumptions, known failure modes and attribution. Charalampos Nadiotis leads the current ROS3-Limassol implementation and manuscript; Christos Paparistodemou is credited for the initial project idea. Other contributors retain their recorded roles and rights.

Civilian use is the programme's proposed mission. Permissions remain specific to each artefact: current software notices and the named manuscript licence must be presented separately. This charter grants no new rights and changes no existing licence.

The initial scope excludes claims to replace ROS, unsupported performance comparisons and unverified deployment claims. PELAOS service offers and customer records have separate planning and evidence records. Expansion should follow a documented user need or research question, a bounded deliverable and evidence sufficient to assess it.

## Adoption questions kept open

- Does the proposed scope cover the intended OpenPELAOS work without claiming other contributors' projects?
- Should the first canonical entry point be the PELAOS documentation or a later separate OpenPELAOS repository?
- Are the evidence requirements practical for each included project?
