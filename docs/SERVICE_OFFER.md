# PELAOS landing-page pilot

Proposed offer for one small business. Price is a hypothesis to validate, not a market benchmark. This document is a quoting aid, not a checkout.

**€250 project fee:** one responsive business page using the client's final text and images; services, location and contact links; title/description/social metadata; one revision; source handover and basic editing instructions.

**Target delivery:** five working days after receiving the agreed deposit and complete content, subject to availability confirmed in the quote.

**Payment proposal:** €125 deposit, €125 after the agreed review and before launch. State the final payable amount and any applicable taxes in the written quote before accepting money. Agree cancellation and refund terms at the same time.

**Separate costs and scope:** domain, hosting, third-party subscriptions, copywriting, logo work, translation, booking systems, payments, accounts, custom apps, extra revisions and continuing maintenance. No third-party purchase without the client's agreement.

## Before accepting a deposit

Confirm the client and decision maker, one-page content, permitted images, preferred contact channel, required language, deadline, revision scope and hosting arrangement. Check project fit and any existing professional obligations. Both parties keep a copy of the accepted scope.

## Delivery and acceptance

Check the agreed content on phone and desktop widths, keyboard access, working contact links, metadata and deployment. Provide a review URL and one consolidated feedback round. Deliver source and a short handover. Do not promise search rankings, leads or sales.

## Economics to measure

At €250 gross project revenue, 8 delivery hours is €31.25 per delivery hour; 12 hours is about €20.83. These figures are before acquisition time, overhead, fees and taxes. Record all hours. If the actual scope exceeds the pilot, revise the next quote or offer rather than doing unbounded work.
